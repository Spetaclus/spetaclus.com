What is Spectacle

A finance layer built on your accounting data

Your accountant keeps the books. Spectacle makes them useful: a fixed monthly delivery of reporting, analysis and advice, shaped around your company and your questions.

  • A tailored monthly report Not a system printout, but a report built around what actually drives your business: performance, variances and cash, explained in plain language.
  • Answers when you need them Specific questions about your finances get specific answers, in writing and on the record, from one adviser who knows your numbers.
  • A fixed monthly fee An ongoing delivery against an agreed scope. You always know what is included, and anything beyond the list is cleared with you first.

How it works

From first meeting to steady delivery

  1. Discovery meeting

    A short online meeting about your company, your numbers and what you need. We do our homework in public sources beforehand, and never ask for anything we can find ourselves.

  2. Onboarding project

    Every engagement starts with an onboarding project: we clean up the data foundation, set up the data flow and build your reporting template. That way the ongoing delivery stands on solid ground from month one.

  3. Ongoing delivery

    A fixed monthly report and follow-up against the agreed scope, in the package that fits your company.

Safe and straightforward

The principles we work by

  • Built on your accountant's work No change of systems or accountant. Spectacle builds on the accounting data you already have, as an addition, not a replacement.
  • Confidentiality and a data processing agreement Client data is treated as confidential, and the processing is governed by a separate data processing agreement.
  • Accountable for the delivery Spectacle is accountable for the quality of what is delivered. The scope is agreed up front, so you know what is included.
  • Predictable economics A fixed monthly fee for the ongoing delivery. Work beyond the agreed scope is always cleared with you before it is done.
